**Riad Afridi Shibly** — Software developer at Evatix, based in Dhaka.

I write mostly Go, TypeScript with detours into Rust, and Swift. My repos lean
toward systems and developer tooling:
[`relay`](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/relay), a simple deployment tool
(also written twice, in Go and
[Rust](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/relay-rs));
[`fj`](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/fj), a `gh`-like CLI for Forgejo;
[`mdgrep`](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/mdgrep), a node-aware grep for
Markdown; [`pixe`](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/pixe), an sxiv-style
very fast image viewer for macOS;
[`diskclean`](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/diskclean) who eats up your
disk space, [`npmclean`](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/npmclean) to delete
your `node_modules` faster and a [BitTorrent
client](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/bittorrent), an [LC3
VM](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/lc3.go), and a [Monkey
interpreter](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/go-monkey) built for the fun
of it.

I came to software from mechanical engineering, and the habit stuck: take
something apart to learn how it works, then rebuild it. That's most of what's
on this profile — [encryption tools](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/enc)
with X25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305,
[ChaCha20-Poly1305](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/chacha20poly1305)
implemented from the paper, [Neovim](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/nvim)
and [terminal configs](https://github.com/riadafridishibly/configs) I keep
tuning, and a long tail of small experiments.

I write occasionally at [riad.run](https://riad.run).
